# Brand Strategy: ÉTHERIA BOTANICALS

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Brand Name:** ÉTHERIA BOTANICALS
- **Tagline:** Cellular Botanical Elixirs & Bio-Active Skincare
- **Positioning:** Ultra-luxury, high-potency bio-botanical skincare formulated at the intersection of alpine plant genetics and clinical cellular dermatological science.
- **Tone of Voice:** Editorial, serene, authoritative, sensory, sophisticated.

## 2. Visual Palette (Design Tokens)
- **Primary Background:** Warm Alabaster (`hsl(40, 20%, 97%)` / `#FBF9F5`)
- **Secondary Background:** Deep Botanical Obsidian (`hsl(150, 15%, 10%)` / `#161B18`)
- **Accent Gold / Bronze:** Muted Botanical Gold (`hsl(38, 45%, 55%)` / `#C5A059`)
- **Text Primary:** Rich Dark Olive Charcoal (`hsl(150, 20%, 12%)` / `#19221C`)
- **Text Muted:** Soft Clay Grey (`hsl(40, 10%, 45%)` / `#78746D`)
- **Surface Elevation:** Translucent Glass Warmth (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`)

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Primary Serif Header:** *Cormorant Garamond* / *Playfair Display* / *Bodoni Moda* (Google Fonts serif - elegant, high-contrast, editorial serif with delicate italics).
- **Primary Sans-Serif Body:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter* / *Outfit* (Modern geometric sans-serif for high legibility).

## 4. Visual World Principles
1. **Organic Contour Line Art:** Delicate, continuous-stroke vector paths in soft gold (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`) weaving through background layers, directly echoing the reference video's subtle vector line movement.
2. **Asymmetric Grid & Staggered Cards:** Staggered product cards and floating image frames creating 2.5D visual depth.
3. **Interactive Circular Badges:** Rotating typography badges ("100% PURE CELLULAR ACTIVES • DERMATOLOGIST FORMULATED") rotating smoothly in response to scroll or idle state.
4. **Cinematic Botanical Imagery:** High-contrast, rich macro photography of botanical drops, dark glass bottles, and radiant skin texture.
