# Skill: UX Research for Skincare Commerce

## Objective
Produce evidence-labeled UX requirements, information architecture, journeys and conversion hypotheses for a premium skincare ecommerce experience.

## Inputs
- `production_artifacts/00_project/Project_Charter.md`
- `production_artifacts/00_project/Current_Brief.md`
- supplied competitor links/research
- supplied visual/video references

## Research scope
Investigate, where evidence is available:
- skincare shopping motivations and concerns
- first-time vs returning customer journeys
- product discovery and category navigation
- ingredient/product education
- routine-building or product pairing
- product detail page comprehension
- mobile shopping behavior
- trust, reviews and proof
- search/filter behavior
- cart and checkout friction
- accessibility and motion considerations

Do not invent interviews, analytics, test results or medical outcomes.

## Visual reference handling
Use the supplied video to identify UX-relevant interaction patterns such as:
- section pacing
- scroll-triggered reveals
- visual hierarchy
- product focus
- transitions between editorial and commerce content

Do not treat visual style alone as UX evidence.

## Deliverable
Write:
`production_artifacts/01_research/UX_Requirements.md`

Include:
- evidence labels
- target users
- primary journeys
- IA/navigation
- usability requirements
- conversion hypotheses
- accessibility requirements
- motion risks
- unresolved questions
- acceptance criteria

## Handoff
End with the Universal Handoff Summary defined in `.agents/agents.md`.
