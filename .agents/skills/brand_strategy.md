# Skill: Brand Strategy for Skincare Commerce

## Objective
Define a coherent premium skincare brand direction that can guide UI, imagery, motion and ecommerce content.

## Inputs
- `production_artifacts/01_research/UX_Requirements.md`
- `production_artifacts/00_project/Project_Charter.md`
- supplied visual/video references

## Responsibilities
Define:
- positioning
- target audience framing
- value proposition
- naming direction if needed
- tone of voice
- visual-world principles
- color direction
- typography direction
- photography/cinematography language
- casting direction
- lighting language
- texture/material language
- composition rules
- campaign territories
- prohibited visual clichés
- skincare claim/truthfulness boundaries

## Important
- Consume UX evidence before final positioning.
- Do not invent clinical evidence or product efficacy claims.
- Keep product facts separate from aesthetic concepts.
- The reference video informs art direction and motion language, but does not define the brand identity by itself.

## Deliverable
Write:
`production_artifacts/02_brand/Brand_Strategy.md`

Also define a concise creative brief suitable for downstream UI, image-generation and motion agents.

## Handoff
Record decisions, assumptions, risks and next dependencies.
